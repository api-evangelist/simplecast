Selling podcast ads means making decisions before you know exactly how future episodes will perform. That makes inventory planning one of the hardest parts of growing podcast revenue. A strong inventory forecast helps publishers look ahead using historical performance, upcoming release plans, and existing campaign commitments to estimate how much ad capacity they are likely to have available.
